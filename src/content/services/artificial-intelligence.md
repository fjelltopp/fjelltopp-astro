## **Overview**

**Artificial intelligence (AI)** enables automation, augmentation, and intelligent decision-making across complex systems. At **Fjelltopp**, we specialize in advising and leading AI initiatives in public health, development, and humanitarian sectors. With hands-on experience in **machine learning**, **computer vision**, and **natural language processing**, we help partners understand what’s possible, make informed choices, and implement meaningful solutions. We combine this cross-cutting technical expertise with **years of frontline public health experience**, ensuring that every AI recommendation is grounded in domain realities and tailored to real-world health system needs.

We don’t just deliver technical tools—we **translate AI potential into strategy**, guiding stakeholders through opportunities, risks, and integration paths that align with their mission.

---

### **Our Services**

- **AI Strategy & Stakeholder Engagement**
  We lead cross-sectoral AI projects from vision to execution. This includes explaining technical opportunities to non-technical stakeholders, aligning initiatives with public health goals, and developing responsible, scalable AI roadmaps.

- **Machine Learning & Predictive Analytics**
  With experience in developing ML models for forecasting, classification, and optimization, we advise on model feasibility, architecture, and data needs—helping teams avoid common pitfalls and unlock real value.

- **Computer Vision**
  We’ve worked with imagery for disease vector detection, environmental analysis, and field data extraction. We guide partners in understanding what CV can realistically deliver in their context, and how to pilot and scale it responsibly.

- **Data Preparation & Annotation**
  We help teams build the datasets their models need—advising on labeling strategies, annotation tools, and quality assurance processes to support accurate, unbiased training data.

- **System Integration & Deployment**
  We ensure that AI integrates with real-world workflows by advising on infrastructure, interoperability (e.g., with DHIS2, CKAN, ODK), and deployment strategies that suit local capacity and connectivity.

- **Ethical Oversight & Lifecycle Support**
  We help organisations think beyond prototypes—putting systems in place to monitor performance, manage bias, retrain with new data, and maintain trust over time.
