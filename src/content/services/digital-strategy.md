## **Turning Vision Into Impact**

Digital health initiatives often fail—not because of poor technology, but because of poor planning. A strong **digital strategy** goes beyond selecting tools. It demands a **holistic understanding** of the ecosystem: the people, the data, the infrastructure, and the institutional realities that shape success.

At **Fjelltopp**, we help governments, UN agencies, and NGOs build strategies that reflect **real-world complexity**. We work closely with partners to:

- Understand the **existing data landscape**—where data is collected, how it's used, and where gaps or duplications exist
- Explore the **potential value of untapped data** and systems not yet integrated
- Identify limitations in **connectivity, digital literacy, workforce capacity**, and **policy frameworks**
- Prioritise interventions that are **feasible, impactful, and sustainable** in the local context

Our approach combines **technical foresight** with **on-the-ground insight**. We lead assessments, co-design sessions, and roadmap development that align digital investments with long-term health goals and operational realities.

Whether designing a national data architecture or improving local reporting workflows, we ensure that your digital transformation is not just visionary—but actionable, inclusive, and built to last.
