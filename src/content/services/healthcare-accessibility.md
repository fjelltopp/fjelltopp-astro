## **From Barriers to Solutions: Making Healthcare Access Measurable and Actionable**

Improving healthcare access requires more than good intentions—it demands **clear data, strategic insights, and actionable tools**. At **Fjelltopp**, we help organisations identify and reduce barriers to care, ensuring services reach those who need them most.

During the Ukrainian refugee crisis, for example, we partnered with **WHO Romania** to integrate national health insurance, clinic, and survey data into real-time dashboards. This allowed authorities to monitor refugee access to vaccination, primary care, and specialist referrals across the country—informing immediate decisions and long-term planning.

---

### **What We Offer**

- **Data-Driven Insights**
  Analyze service use patterns and population coverage to detect where and for whom access is failing.

- **Real-Time Monitoring**
  Deploy dashboards and alerts to track shifting access challenges, especially in crisis or mobile populations.

- **Policy & Program Support**
  Use evidence to target outreach, deploy mobile clinics, and allocate resources where they're needed most.

- **Capacity Building**
  Train national and local teams on data tools, analysis techniques, and dashboard maintenance to ensure sustainability.
