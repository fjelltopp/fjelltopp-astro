## **Take Control of Your Data Distribution with a Health Data Catalogue**

Ministries of Health manage **vast amounts of data**, accessed by **diverse stakeholders**. These datasets must be stored **securely**, with **controlled access**, **clear dissemination**, and **robust interoperability**.

Using **data catalogues in healthcare** settings saves time, reduces costs, and boosts innovation and productivity among staff and external partners. We specialise in implementing **CKAN**, an **open-source**, **feature-rich** data catalogue trusted globally by governments for **efficient data sharing**.

Drawing on our work with ministries and international agencies, we tailor CKAN implementations to diverse operational requirements—ensuring **secure publication**, **stakeholder collaboration**, and **API-driven interoperability**.
