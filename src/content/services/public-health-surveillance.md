## **From Signals to Systems: Designing Surveillance That Works**

Effective public health surveillance underpins early detection, rapid response, and long-term system resilience. At **Fjelltopp**, we bring deep expertise in the design and implementation of **routine, event-based, syndromic, and indicator-based** surveillance systems—each tailored to different data needs, risks, and operational realities.

Whether strengthening routine reporting, introducing community-based alert systems, or integrating background environmental and laboratory data, we help partners design strategies that are **fit for purpose**, **technically sound**, and **operationally feasible**.

Our team works with governments, UN agencies, and research institutions to modernize surveillance architectures—bridging health, animal, and environmental sectors—while aligning with global standards and national policy priorities. With a combination of **technical depth** and **strategic vision**, we support everything from data schema design to multi-stakeholder coordination.

---

### **What We Offer**

- **Strategic Surveillance Design**
  We work with ministries and partners to define surveillance goals, system architecture, and reporting requirements—advising on the right tools, indicators, and governance models.

- **Multi-Sectoral Coordination**
  We lead stakeholder engagement processes across human, animal, and environmental health sectors—supporting One Health surveillance strategies and harmonized data flows.

- **Custom System Implementation**
  From DHIS2 Tracker programs to mobile data collection apps (ODK, KoboToolbox), we configure surveillance tools that support case reporting, contact tracing, alerts, and automated analytics.

- **Capacity Building & Handover**
  We provide tailored training, clear documentation, and user-friendly tools that enable sustainable in-country ownership and ongoing system maintenance.
