<p>
  Dr George Alexander Gazis is Associate Professor in Greek Literature and
  Philosophy (Ethics) and Deputy Head of Department at Durham University.
</p>
<p>
  Dr Gazis has worked extensively on the meta-ethics of mortality in ancient and
  contemporary societies, seeking to determine the impact that perceived
  concepts of morality have on individual and collective decisions about the
  best way to live one’s life.
</p>
<p>
  He has been a lead reviewer for the Greater Manchester NHS Ethics committee
  for several years. His responsibilities include vetting research applications
  by institutions or individual medical professionals, evaluating benefits
  versus risks, and interviewing proposers to ensure adherence to the UK
  government’s Medical Ethics guidelines. Dr Gazis has also worked as an ethics
  consultant on major medical research projects, including test and controlled
  trials, and is currently collaborating with the Wolfson Institute for Medical
  Humanities at Durham on a research proposal concerning the use of literature
  and art as a non-invasive approach to addressing mental conditions.
</p>
