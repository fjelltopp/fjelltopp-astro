<p>
  Dr. John Haskew is a medical doctor and public health specialist with over 20
  years of global experience in health emergency response, healthcare systems
  strengthening, and digital health. He has served in leadership roles with WHO,
  IFRC, and UNHCR, managing public health interventions across Africa, Eastern
  Europe, and the Middle East.
</p>
<p>
  Dr. Haskew holds degrees in medicine and zoology from the University of
  Cambridge and a Master’s in Public Health from the London School of Hygiene &
  Tropical Medicine. His career has spanned field operations in refugee health,
  disease surveillance, emergency coordination, and innovative use of health
  information systems in crisis settings.
</p>
<p>
  He has worked extensively in conflict-affected and fragile contexts, including
  Romania’s response to the Ukraine crisis, Somalia, Jordan, and Sierra Leone,
  and has contributed to WHO’s emergency health leadership and preparedness
  strategies. His technical and strategic expertise is complemented by a strong
  academic background and a commitment to equitable, evidence-based global
  health practice.
</p>
